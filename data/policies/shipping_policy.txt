SHOPEASY SHIPPING POLICY
Policy ID: SHIPPING-001

1. Standard shipping normally takes 3 to 7 business days after dispatch.
2. Processing orders have not yet been dispatched. A carrier tracking number may not be available until dispatch.
3. Shipped orders may display a carrier and tracking status when those details are available in the order record.
4. Delivery dates displayed before dispatch are estimates, not guaranteed commitments.
5. If a package is more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date, the customer may ask support to investigate.
6. Support must use the actual order record for an individual order's status and delivery details.
This policy does not guarantee compensation for shipping delays.
