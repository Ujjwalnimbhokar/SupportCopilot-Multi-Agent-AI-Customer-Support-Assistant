SHOPEASY WARRANTY POLICY
Policy ID: WARRANTY-001

1. Eligible electronics carry a 12-month limited warranty beginning on the recorded delivery date.
2. The warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use.
3. Accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, and normal cosmetic wear are excluded from standard warranty coverage.
4. Warranty claims require order details and a description of the defect. Support may request photographs or diagnostic evidence.
5. Warranty eligibility must be reviewed before a repair, replacement, or refund is promised.
6. A warranty claim is separate from a standard return request. Customers should not assume warranty coverage extends the standard return window.
The final warranty decision belongs to ShopEasy support.
