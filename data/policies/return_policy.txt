SHOPEASY RETURN POLICY
Policy ID: RETURN-001

1. Laptops may be returned within 15 calendar days of delivery.
2. Other electronics may be returned within 10 calendar days of delivery.
3. The return period starts on the recorded delivery date, not the order date.
4. Products must include accessories and original packaging unless the item arrived damaged.
5. A return request does not guarantee approval. ShopEasy must verify the order, delivery date, product category, and applicable policy.
6. Items outside the stated return window are not eligible under this standard return policy.
This document does not define refund processing times or warranty repair eligibility.
