SHOPEASY REFUND POLICY
Policy ID: REFUND-001

1. A refund can be considered after the return or damage claim has been verified.
2. A product reported as damaged on arrival may qualify for a refund when the customer reports the damage within 30 calendar days of delivery and provides proof of damage.
3. Proof may include clear photographs of the damaged product or packaging, or another form of evidence accepted by ShopEasy support.
4. A refund must not be issued until the order owner, order amount, eligibility, and any required evidence have been checked.
5. Refund amounts must not exceed the verified refundable amount for the order.
6. Requests for refunds above USD 50 require approval from a human support agent before the refund is issued.
7. Requests at or below USD 50 must still pass all eligibility and ownership checks. The threshold does not automatically make a refund eligible.
8. If the applicable policy does not establish eligibility, escalate the case to support rather than promising a refund.
This policy does not authorize bypassing identity checks or human approval.
